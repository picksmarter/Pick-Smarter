How to Read the Pick Smarter Dashboard

You don't need to understand any of the math to use the dashboard well. Here's what's actually on the page and what to do with it, top to bottom.

1. Quick Read

At the top of every weekly report are three cards: Best Leverage Pick, Safest Favorite, and Best Upset Opportunity. If you only have thirty seconds, this is the section to read. It's the same information as the full slate below, distilled down to the three matchups most worth your attention.

2. "What do these numbers mean?"

A short, collapsible reference for the four terms used everywhere on the page: Win %, Public %, Leverage, and the Pick Smarter Pick. Open it any time you want a refresher — it's intentionally kept out of the way otherwise.

3. All Matchups

The full weekly slate, one row (or card, on mobile) per game. For each matchup:

  • Pick Smarter Pick — the team we recommend, shown first and most prominently. This is the single most important thing on each row.
  • Win % — our fair estimate of that team's chance to win.
  • Public % — how often the public is actually picking that team.
  • Leverage — the gap between the two, shown with a plus/minus sign and an icon so it doesn't rely on color alone.
  • A short explanation of why — not just the numbers, but what they mean together.

What to actually do with it

Read the recommendation first. If you want to know why, read the short explanation. If you want the full picture, look at the three numbers. That order — what, then why, then the numbers — is intentional. You're never required to go further than the first line if you don't want to.

For the concepts behind the numbers, start with What Is Leverage in Pick'em? and Win Probability vs. Public Pick Percentage. For the full calculation, see Methodology.
